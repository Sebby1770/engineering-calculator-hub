Calculations that stay transparent and connected

Engineering Calculator Hub is built for students, engineers, makers, and anyone who needs dependable answers without rebuilding the same spreadsheet. Today the library covers 67 calculators across 13 categories — every one with its formula, a worked example, and FAQs. Results can be collected into a local-first project sheet with assumptions and exports for review.
